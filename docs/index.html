About this Website

Our website explores dialogue in the first 40 chapters of the manhwa Solo Leveling. We collected the dialogue and organized data for each chapter, including the number of speech bubbles and the number of unique characters who speak. By comparing these counts across chapters, visitors can explore how dialogue and character participation change as the story develops.

About this Manhwa

Solo Leveling is a Korean manhwa about Sung Jinwoo, a weak hunter who risks his life fighting monsters to support his family. In a world where hunters enter dangerous dungeons through mysterious gates, Jinwoo barely survives a deadly encounter and gains access to a mysterious “System” that allows him to grow stronger by completing quests and leveling up. As he develops powerful abilities, including the power to command an army of shadows, he uncovers the truth behind the gates and becomes involved in a war that threatens humanity. The story explores perseverance, personal growth, and the responsibility that comes with power.
